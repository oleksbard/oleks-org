This is the first note on my new site. I've been meaning to carve out a small,
personal corner of the web; somewhere to keep games and side projects,
photography, and the occasional short write-up. This is that corner.

The stack is intentionally boring:

- **Next.js 16** (App Router, RSC) with **React 19** and **TypeScript**.
- **styled-components** for styles, with SSR wired up so there's no FOUC.
- **MDX** for content — notes and game pages are just files in the repo.
- **Vercel** for hosting.

More soon.
