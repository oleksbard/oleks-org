This site has a notes section. Short updates, changelog-style — no long
essays. The MDX pipeline is wired up with `@next/mdx`, with frontmatter
parsed by `gray-matter` and the body rendered via a dynamic import.

What's working:

- Notes are reverse-chron by date.
- Frontmatter goes through a typed loader in `lib/content/notes.ts`.
- The body inherits typography from the shared `<Prose>` wrapper.

More notes when there's something to say.
