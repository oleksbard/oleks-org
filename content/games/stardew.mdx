Type "ancient fruit" or "community center" or "Sturgeon"; anything from
Stardew Valley and get a clean answer back, not a wall of links to chase.
A search box for the game's entire vocabulary that understands what you
meant, not just what you typed.

## What it does

- One box. Items, characters, quests, events, recipes — all answered in
  plain language.
- Installs on phone or desktop(PWA). Opens from the home screen, behaves like a
  native app, works on slow connections.

## How it's built

The interesting part isn't the search — it's the AI/ML layer sitting in
front of it. A plain query like "best money maker" gets translated into
specific game terms ("Wine", "Ancient Fruit", "Truffle Oil") before anything
is looked up. The candidates come back, the same model re-ranks them by
relevance, and the top hits are enriched with their infoboxes and images.

For one-shot factual questions — "when is Linus's birthday", "where do you
catch Sturgeon", "how much does a Crystalarium cost"; a separate extraction
pass pulls the bare answer out of the top result and surfaces it above the
summary.

## Stack

- **Next.js** + **React**, App Router.
- **TypeScript** end-to-end.
- **Tailwind** for styling.
- **OpenAI** for the language-model re-rank.
- **Serwist** for the PWA layer — service worker, installable shell.
- **Vitest** for tests.
